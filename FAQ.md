# Zyrex FAQ

**Is Zyrex an independent network?**
Yes. Zyrex has its own genesis, network magic, address prefixes and chain state.
It uses eUTXO, programmable contracts and Autolykos v2.

**How is the block subsidy distributed?**
Miners receive 90% and the team receives 10%. The emission contract and native
consensus enforce the payments. Integer rounding goes to the miner.

**Is there a premine?**
No spendable allocation exists before mining. The initial reserve is locked under
the emission contract and released only through validated block subsidies.

**Is mainnet available?**
No. The public testnet is live; mainnet has not launched.
